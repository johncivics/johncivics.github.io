# Privacy Policy

**Subito** · subitoapp.ca  
Last updated: May 2026

---

## What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy describes how Subito collects and uses information on subitoapp.ca. This policy applies to the waitlist site only. A separate, more detailed policy will be published when the Subito app launches.

---

## What We Collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect:

- **Your email address** — the only personal information we ask for

We do not collect your name, location, payment information, or any other personal data through the waitlist.

We may also collect standard technical information automatically, such as browser type and pages visited, through analytics tools. This data is aggregated and not linked to your email address.

---

## How We Use Your Email

We use your email address to:

- Confirm your place on the waitlist
- Send you updates about Subito's development
- Notify you when the app is available

We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.

---

## Email Service Provider

We use a third-party email service to send waitlist communications. Your email address is stored with that provider on our behalf. We choose providers that maintain appropriate security standards.

---

## Your Rights

You can:

- **Unsubscribe** at any time using the link in any email we send
- **Request deletion** of your email from our list by emailing us at hello@subitoapp.ca

We will process removal requests within a reasonable time.

---

## Data Retention

We keep your email address on our waitlist until you unsubscribe or request deletion. If Subito does not launch, we will delete all waitlist data.

---

## Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted their email, please contact us and we will remove it promptly.

---

## Residents of Canada

If you are in Canada, you have rights under the *Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act* (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation, including the right to access personal information we hold about you and to request corrections. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@subitoapp.ca.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our practices change. We will update the "last updated" date at the top of the page when we do. For significant changes, we will send a notice to the email address on file if it is practical to do so.

---

## Contact

Privacy questions or requests: **hello@subitoapp.ca**
